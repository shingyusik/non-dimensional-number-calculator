Guides and calculators by field

Calculators, basic guides and calculation notes, grouped by field.

This page lists the site’s calculators, the basic guide for each dimensionless number, and the calculation notes, grouped by field. Calculator pages let you calculate while checking the formula, units and how to read the result. Basic guides walk through one example to show how inputs are chosen. Calculation notes chain several numbers on one input set and check that they agree.

Numbers are grouped by the problems they are mainly used for. The Péclet number also applies to mass transfer, but it sits under heat transfer because this site’s calculator takes thermal diffusivity; notes that span several fields are listed under each of them.

Fluid flow

Balances between inertia, viscosity and gravity, plus periodic motion inside a flow: pipe flow, open channels, scale-model tests and vortex shedding behind cylinders.

Calculators

  • Reynolds number (Re): Inertia versus viscosity, with circular-pipe regime guidance.
  • Froude number (Fr): Flow speed versus gravity-wave speed: channels and ships.
  • Strouhal number (St): Oscillation period versus transit time: vortex shedding.

Basic guides

  • Reynolds number guide: Re for a pipe example and the limits of the 2300 and 4000 thresholds.
  • Froude number guide: Fr from a channel depth and the subcritical/supercritical split.
  • Strouhal number guide: St from vortex shedding behind a cylinder.

Calculation notes

  • Cross-check Re, Pr and Pe for pipe flow: One input set through three calculators, checking that Pe = Re × Pr.
  • Can a scale model match both Re and Fr?: Two candidate speeds for a 1:10 model and why they conflict.
  • Pipe pressure loss: from conservation laws to Poiseuille flow: Mass and energy balances show why the same Re can give different pressure losses.

Heat transfer

Separate ratios for heat carried by the flow, heat diffusing through the fluid, and heat exchanged at a surface.

Calculators

  • Nusselt number (Nu): Convection versus conduction over the same length.
  • Prandtl number (Pr): Momentum versus thermal diffusivity; a fluid property.
  • Péclet number (Pe): Advection versus diffusion, for heat or mass.

Basic guides

  • Nusselt number guide: Turning a given h into Nu, and mean versus local values.
  • Prandtl number guide: Pr from properties at one temperature.
  • Péclet number guide: Pe from thermal diffusivity, distinguished from the mass Pe.

Calculation notes

  • Thermal and mass diffusion: the distinct roles of Pr, Sc, Pe and Nu: Diffusion lengths versus transit times, and where a heat-transfer coefficient comes from.
  • Cross-check Re, Pr and Pe for pipe flow: One input set through three calculators, checking that Pe = Re × Pr.

Mass transfer

Spreading of a dissolved or gaseous species. The molecular diffusivity D of that species takes the place of thermal diffusivity.

Calculators

  • Schmidt number (Sc): Momentum versus mass diffusivity.

Basic guides

  • Schmidt number guide: Sc for a solute in water and what D represents.

Calculation notes

  • Thermal and mass diffusion: the distinct roles of Pr, Sc, Pe and Nu: Diffusion lengths versus transit times, and where a heat-transfer coefficient comes from.

Interfaces

Droplets, bubbles and sprays, where the shape of the boundary between two fluids depends on inertia versus surface tension.

Calculators

  • Weber number (We): Inertia versus surface tension: drops and sprays.

Basic guides

  • Weber number guide: We for a water drop and the choice of L.

Calculation notes

  • Surface tension and unsteady flow: connect force ratios to time ratios: We, Re, Fr and St on one length scale; curvature pressure versus capillary time.

Gases and compressibility

Two separate checks for gases: whether density changes matter (Mach number) and whether molecular length scales can be ignored (Knudsen number).

Calculators

  • Mach number (Ma): Flow speed versus speed of sound: compressibility.
  • Knudsen number (Kn): Mean free path versus device size: the continuum check.

Basic guides

  • Mach number guide: Ma from speed and sound speed; free-stream versus local.
  • Knudsen number guide: Kn from mean free path and channel size.

Calculation notes

  • Choosing gas-flow models: check Mach and Knudsen numbers separately: Compressibility and molecular length scales, checked separately with isentropic relations.
